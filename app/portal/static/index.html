Disconnect A TikTok Advertiser

Disconnecting removes Inlet’s stored authorization for this connection, stops imports, and clears unfinished pages and pending analysis. Historical campaigns, metrics, reports, goals and audit records remain without further updates. Running TikTok campaigns and spending are unchanged. This does not delete TikTok data or revoke access for your other connections.

Connect Meta Ads

Disconnecting removes this workspace’s stored Meta authorization and unfinished imports. Historical reports, campaigns, metrics and goal history remain in Inlet without new imports. Pending Meta connection attempts in this workspace are canceled. Running Meta campaigns and spending are unchanged; this does not revoke access elsewhere or delete data in Meta.

Connect Google Ads

Review Google’s requested permissions, then choose an authorized advertising account. Inlet imports reporting data; connecting does not authorize advertising changes or spending. Manager accounts cannot be connected directly.

Disconnect A Google Ads Account

Disconnecting removes Inlet’s stored authorization for this connection, stops its future imports, and discards unfinished import pages and pending analysis deliveries. Previously imported campaigns, metrics, reports, goal history and audit records remain in Inlet; they will no longer receive updates from this connection. Reconnect before relying on current reporting or reviewing account-bound actions. This does not delete Google data, stop running campaigns, change spending, or revoke Google access for your other Inlet connections.
