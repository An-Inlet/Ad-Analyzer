    Session Automation

    Runs only while the session that started it remains valid. Signing out stops future changes. Stopping cannot undo a change already sent.

    Automation may adjust existing campaign budgets only within your exact consented spending mandate and an available registered policy. It cannot raise your overall spending limit or create campaigns or creative.

    Status And Recovery

    After an uncertain response, refresh status and select the saved run. Inlet will not repeat Start or Stop automatically. Stopping does not cancel external advertising, erase financial obligations or prove spending has stopped.

    Spending Mandate

    These are explicit UTC spending-period instants, not your store-local goal dates. Your account time zone is shown above. Inlet does not convert goal dates into spending consent.

    Aggressiveness never raises your overall spending limit. Budget decreases, pause, cancellation and new consent do not reset spending or release uncertain exposure.

    Exact Budget Or Pause

    The existing daily or lifetime mode stays unchanged. No resume, creative edits or new campaigns are included.

    Repeat requires qualified closure and transition evidence for the selected original run. A successful status read alone does not supply that evidence.

    Owned Execution And Recovery
