Review Local Closure Effects

Confirming irreversibly removes Inlet’s locally held connection credentials and pending authorizations for this workspace. It disables new workspace work and requests cancellation of local automation. Existing work may still be in flight; stopping it has not been verified.

Historical reporting, media, goals, financial reservations and audit records are retained. Full closure and data erasure remain blocked until outstanding obligations and retention decisions are resolved.

External advertising campaigns and provider permissions are not changed or verified revoked. Billing and subscriptions are not canceled. Review them separately with the providers. This action does not close your global Inlet identity or other workspaces.
