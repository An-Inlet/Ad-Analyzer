Build And Download Archive

Each download builds a new snapshot. Your browser saves the TAR archive; Inlet does not retain a copy. Refresh status after downloading to see generation results. Generation does not confirm that your download arrived.

What The Archive Contains

The archive includes a manifest, supported workspace records and verified supplied media. The manifest identifies missing data and intentional exclusions. Session credentials, provider grants and global identity records are excluded. Some legacy records cannot yet be attributed to a workspace, so this is not a complete export of every stored record.

Missing or corrupt media prevents an archive from being built. Large workspaces may exceed the bounded export limits. This export does not close your workspace, erase data, cancel billing or stop advertising at a provider.
