# Meta And TikTok Native Exposure Eligibility

2026-10-06. Bounded official-document/source assessment only; no provider API calls, credentials, new runtime contracts, qualification registration or policy changes. Related: [Google assessment](google-native-exposure-qualification.md), [joint-envelope proposal](google-joint-envelope-proposal.md), and [existing social optimization adapters](../inlet-social-optimization-adapters.md).

## Finding

Neither reviewed launch profile currently has a qualified strict-ceiling source or an authoritative original-request finality source. Actual API transports and visible native cap fields do not fill those gaps. TikTok provides useful first-party descriptions of whole-account and lifetime controls; Meta's official SDK supplies concrete field/update schemas, but the relevant help/API pages were inaccessible in this bounded review. That retrieval limitation is recorded rather than replaced with third-party claims.

The launch adapter subset remains manual Meta OUTCOME_SALES/AUCTION and regular TikTok WEB_CONVERSIONS/WEBSITE, with independent child budgets or explicitly owned campaign pools. Unsupported sharing, scheduling, automation and smart-campaign combinations remain blocked. No resume, account-cap mutation, new campaign or creative authority is added.

## Accounting And Reporting

TikTok's Net Cost (Delayed) is explicitly net of advertising credits/coupons; its help states that the metric may take up to 11 hours to populate and shows zero before availability. The described metric is tied to Sales reporting, not a universal watermark for every supported Business API report. Do not promote the stated delay into a complete, immutable accounting cutoff, treat placeholder zero as final spend, or apply this net metric to gross spending without a qualified mapping. [Net Cost metric](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/introducing-net-cost-delayed-metric?lang=en).

Budget Manager's credit example distinguishes configured cap from available credit: its example pauses at the configured cost amount even when more credit exists. That does not resolve taxes, fees, later refunds/adjustments or the precise relationship between all API spend fields. A source still needs a versioned accounting-basis mapping and reconciliation evidence. The account cap should never be inferred from cash plus credit arithmetic. [Budget Manager](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-use-budget-manager?lang=en).

Meta's SDK field names alone do not establish whether `amount_spent`, Insights spend, balance and cap counting share a period or adjustment basis. No current primary evidence obtained in this review proves a compatible non-reversing subtraction rule or universal reporting completion bound. Therefore `cap - amount_spent`, ledger high-water reduction, or a guessed delay reserve remains unsupported. No Meta daily-overdelivery percentage is assumed here.

For both providers, a future qualified whole-window cap should be composed as the [separate joint-envelope proposal](google-joint-envelope-proposal.md) describes, not disguised as independent `U=0` or `F=0` components. Until that mode is reviewed and backed by actual provider evidence, all new diagnostics remain explicitly unqualified/null-bounded. Existing high-water, all other account exposure and prior-period holds remain preserved.

## External Writers, Pending Changes And Finality

TikTok documents automated rules that can increase budgets, and Smart+ budget scheduling can arrange future increases. Smart+ is outside the initial profile, but complete portfolio qualification still cannot ignore unsupported sibling campaigns that consume the same account cap. User/API writers, automated rules, pending changes and cap resets must all be covered by a real preventive boundary. [Automated rules](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/automated-rules?lang=en), [budget scheduling](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-budget-scheduling?lang=en).

No reviewed source establishes a preventive cap lock or immutable exclusive-writer fence for either provider. A role listing, dedicated account, polling loop, change history export or customer promise is not that guarantee. Inlet database locks serialize Inlet workers only. This is a current evidence gap, not a claim that no provider mechanism could ever exist.

For TikTok, the official generic SDK response calls `request_id` a request log identifier. The regular campaign update model provides advertiser/campaign identity and budget fields but no documented idempotency or terminal-request lookup semantics. Treat the ID as diagnostic correlation; do not use it as exactly-once, cancellation, no-later-effect or safe-retry proof. [Response schema](https://github.com/tiktok/tiktok-business-api-sdk/blob/f809c396520df2d7b201a9ccc5378d822b728ed3/python_sdk/docs/InlineResponse200.md), [campaign update schema](https://github.com/tiktok/tiktok-business-api-sdk/blob/f809c396520df2d7b201a9ccc5378d822b728ed3/java_sdk/docs/CampaignUpdateBody.md).

The reviewed Meta campaign/ad-set update schemas likewise do not supply documented original-request terminality, conditional resource revision or idempotent retry guarantees. This bounded negative finding does not prove those features universally absent; it means none can be relied upon in the current adapters. Acknowledgement, configured PAUSED/DISABLE, old-value readback after timeout, elapsed time or request success callback cannot close an unresolved command. The repeat-optimization protocol still requires authoritative predecessor closure plus a full transitional exposure envelope before atomically superseding its hold.

## Concrete Evidence Acquisition Package

Before another qualified-source implementation, obtain a provider-owned, versioned specification or approved evidence capture covering:

1. **Native cap semantics:** exact profile eligibility, amount/currency precision, window and reset origin, whether charged or served cost is bounded, exceptions/adjustments, delivery-stop behavior, and effective versus pending configuration.
2. **Readable native schema:** Meta account/campaign/ad-set cap counters and history; TikTok Budget Manager effective cap/reset/history and Business Center funding relationships. The official TikTok SDK exposes paginated `/advertiser/balance/get/` described as balance and budget, but its generic response does not establish all required fields or semantics. [Business Center API](https://github.com/tiktok/tiktok-business-api-sdk/blob/f809c396520df2d7b201a9ccc5378d822b728ed3/python_sdk/docs/BCApi.md). No access expansion is authorized by this research.
3. **Complete obligation set:** exact current account generations, all campaigns/children including paused/scheduled/deleted historical liabilities, native owners, shared controls, all other mandate accounts and held attempts/reservations. Missing/truncated/ambiguous inventory is not a zero.
4. **Writer prevention:** actual enforcement against cap increases, resets, replacements, new uncovered owners and automated/scheduled changes throughout the full exposure window. Detection after a change is insufficient.
5. **Original-request finality:** exact endpoint/version evidence for terminality or no-later-effect, including timeout after acceptance, retry behavior, retention and late processing; one request's closure must not be confused with settled reporting.
6. **Accounting compatibility:** immutable versioned monetary basis, credits/refunds/taxes/fees rules and period attribution compatible with the ledger high-water. No subtraction from cap without a separate proved non-reversing basis.

Independent work can implement bounded native diagnostic reads with explicit missing-evidence reasons after their response schemas are confirmed. It must not silently interpret SDK omission as false/zero or register a qualified source. First deterministic cases should cover cap reset/increase after snapshot, pending automatic increase, hidden child/other account, budget decrease after earlier spend, delayed zero report, credits, shared balance, original timeout with late effect, and previous-period held exposure. This assessment does not introduce those runtime contracts.

## Owner Questions And Access Limits

The owner monetary-basis decision remains net billed media versus gross served media versus all-in cash. Preserve the strict approved ceiling while unresolved. Ask whether narrower launch account configurations are acceptable **only after** demonstrating an actual enforceable mechanism and its limitations. A best-effort guarantee would be a separate product-policy decision, not implied approval.
