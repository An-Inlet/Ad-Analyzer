  cells through actual saved-input generation. It is not the exact mixed-provider
  Q09 graph. `selected_composer.observation_text` preserves requested dates, local
  timezone/currency, selected native event, metric family and present/absent days;
  partial observations never become full-window zero. Traffic partial spend is
  explicitly an observed subtotal.
- `selected_plan_bundle.supported` recognizes only the exact Google v24 selected
  action producer/profile and same-advertiser action resource. The adapter uses

  unknown defaults. Google `selected_acquisition.py` currently emits unknown

2. Review separate provider-specific selected-event/attribution raw contracts,
   including exact query, ownership, family, original attribution provenance,
   pagination/completion and missing-vs-zero semantics. Meta presence may be reused

   must cover account currency, closed local interval/coverage, event semantic

   Do not introduce that interface merely to manufacture passing fixtures.

   retaining different purchase/lead and attribution facts in document/export.
