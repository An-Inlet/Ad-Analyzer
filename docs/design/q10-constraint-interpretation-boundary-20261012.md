# Q10 Constrained Creative Interpretation Boundary

## Current Evidence

`compose_creative_proposals` defers every nonempty global constraints tuple. Empty
account preparation also requires no global constraints. Product restrictions are
retained verbatim in briefs and production instructions, but this is not semantic
interpretation or enforcement. The actual v5 Q10 common-basis fixture demonstrates
owned exclusions/fixed allocations/capacity/product restrictions and separately
demonstrates global `No discount claims.` deferral. Preserve these historical policy
results. V3 stays default; v6 remains selectable but unqualified with its open incident.

## Shared Interfaces And Candidate Implementation

Use a versioned pure interpretation result with:

- Exact input-revision digest, original ordered constraint tuple and interpreter
  contract identifier. Never rewrite or silently move the customer's constraints.
- One interpretation entry per source index: exact original text, finite recognized
  rule identifier or unresolved reason. No inferred extra restriction or omission.
- Derived disposition: no constraints, fully recognized, or unresolved. Any unknown
  or mixed recognized/unknown input defers composition. Never ignore the unknown part.
  The implementation explicitly adds a separate ordered product-restriction tuple
  and entries. Fully recognized applies only to these two exact retained tuples;
  unknown product restrictions also defer. It does not qualify external assets.
  Duplicate inputs retain separate source
  entries even if identical production obligations can later be deduplicated.
- Explicit production obligations for every recognized rule. Interpretations are
  customer-instruction provenance, not measured product facts or approvals.

First proposed recognized literal: `No discount claims.` -> `exclude_discount_claims`.
Use exact matching initially, with no substring/fuzzy matching, lowercasing or model
interpretation. Case, punctuation, negation and additional qualifiers remain unresolved
until separately specified. A finite exact vocabulary is a bounded initial capability,
not general natural-language understanding or full Q10 acceptance.

The rule must constrain actual proposed hooks, captions, offers, storyboard and
production instructions. Implementation preflight must enumerate every public-copy
output field, including customer-facing CTA/objective fields where applicable.
Merely appending the original sentence is insufficient.
For this rule, avoid generating price/savings/discount/promotion copy. Omit numeric
price and customer-asserted promotional claims from public-copy suggestions, retain
original facts in provenance, and require review before publication. Do not interpolate
unreviewed product names/facts into suggested public copy when they could contain a
promotion: identify the product internally, and use fixed neutral public-copy templates
or explicitly defer that copy. This is intentionally conservative; it does not
establish semantic compliance of existing image pixels, audio or external assets.
Asset inspection and rights/placement/customer approval remain required.

## Composition And Compatibility Join

Do not remove the global-constraint guard in the shared v5/v6 composer. Introduce
interpretation-aware composition behind a separately versioned policy contract and
exact policy digest, with no registry/default activation in the pure package. Preserve
old input/document bytes and digests. Any later document extension needs a reader
floor, strict optional serialization and retained proof binding; design that join
before changing stored documents. Do not reuse v6 selection as a back door around
its reliability gate. This contract makes no decision to activate a new policy.

The candidate uses exact v7 policy/generator recognition and outer plan-version.v4
with mandatory input-bound interpretation. V1/v2/v3 omit the absent field and retain
their semantics. V4 requires complete typed provenance across capture pins if any
pin is typed; it does not fabricate captures for accounts without them. Settings and
runtime selection remain closed to v7. Real-service API fixtures explicitly inject
candidate policy/media dependencies; they do not replace validators or composers.

The v7 neutral template applies to all its creative proposals, including unconstrained
inputs. Public-copy fields are brief hooks, offer, objective, copy directions and
storyboard captions. Manual steps, rationale, hypothesis, visual/capture instructions,
landing-page constraints and review checklist also avoid arbitrary product strings.
Original name/price/facts remain in original inputs and retained creative basis. No
empty-account creative fallback or unselected-image substitution is introduced.

The composer must revalidate the original input binding and enforce all obligations
before producing a substantive proposal. Exclusions (including children), fixed
allocations, total ceiling, asset capacity, account ownership, image evidence and
review-only authority remain independent invariants. Known constraints must never
increase spending or grant publication permission. Unknown constraints retain the
existing actionable deferral and original text. No model calls are necessary for the
finite first rule; no provider calls or advertising writes belong in this package.

## Finite Acceptance Sequence

1. Pure interpretation: exact recognized/no-constraint/unknown/mixed/duplicate input
   cases, order/text/index preservation, binding and unsafe-copy refusal. Pin negative
   language and punctuation variants; no permissive fallback.
2. Pure constrained production: one owned Meta image proposal and one TikTok video
   proposal, with restrictions reflected in each generated public-copy field. Include
   adversarial product names/facts containing promotional text, unknown constraints,
   excluded targets, exhausted capacity and zero budget. Verify exact original facts
   remain provenance and the public-copy restrictions are enforced, not just echoed.
3. Compatibility: old policy outputs/digests unchanged; no new registry/default
   selection; retained proof/reparse/export and browser read contracts explicit.
4. Only after these interfaces close, qualify the actual customer API and database
   common-basis join on SQLite then PostgreSQL, history/export/isolation/revocation/
   erasure as applicable, with no advertising or financial writes. Browser display
   must distinguish original instruction, bounded interpretation and unresolved parts.

This contract does not establish qualification. Candidate code and its exact frozen
test results must be recorded separately; full Q10 and live verification require
broader acceptance beyond the finite first rule and synthetic provider fixtures.
