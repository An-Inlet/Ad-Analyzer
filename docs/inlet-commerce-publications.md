# Commerce Publications And Privacy Fences

B17 first producer package, based on PR130 0cf6706. Migration0051 descends from0047. No live provider requests, outcome-result persistence, maturity policy, closure disposition, or new spending authority.

`CommerceSyncService.page` now holds the workspace fence before native/store locks. Only a successful terminal page writes an aggregate publication in the same transaction as completion. Earlier pages never expose a mixed-page publication. The payload freezes store-local daily net merchandise sales, currency, timezone, source/API/definition and import-window basis, source revision, generation/privacy epoch, predecessor digest and as-of watermark. Missing history is not zero; successful complete historical reconciliation establishes zero partitions. Updated-at inclusive source ranges are explicitly separate from store-local financial days. A current partial local day is excluded. Amounts are integer micros; payloads contain no customer/order/CRM IDs. Full source identity and financial computation remain in the existing ledger.

Each source has monotonic generation and privacy epoch, distinct from data revision. Rebind, source erasure and disconnect delete publication payloads/digests and clear history qualification. Retention deletes source-wide publications conservatively when any source retention removes data; history coverage can remain qualified for the retained range. New publications after invalidation re-establish history through a full read. Existing installations receive no fabricated completion at migration time.

Customer GET routes:
- `/api/customer/v1/workspaces/{workspace_id}/commerce/stores/{store_id}/publications/latest`
- `/api/customer/v1/workspaces/{workspace_id}/commerce/stores/{store_id}/publications/{publication_id}`

Reads recheck active workspace/source ownership under the workspace fence, validate epoch/generation and content digest, and reject publications whose represented range exceeds current retention. Responses are no-store, copied from the frozen payload, with explicit in-progress/staleness indicators. They always say comparison unavailable because Shopify maturity remains unqualified. Store-total observations are not ad attribution, causal lift, or optimization proof. There is no customer input path for supplying purported measurement evidence.

Privacy maintenance still works for closing workspaces. Customer reads and new publications do not. Source erasure continues its existing authorized deletion behavior; workspace closure continues retaining history under its unset disposition policy. The retention runner now processes one workspace transaction at a time, locking workspace before nonce/native/store cleanup. No new deletion policy was introduced.

`app.commerce.evidence.workspace_privacy_stamp(db, workspace_id)` is the frozen export integration helper: bounded-memory SHA-256 of the ordered owned store set and its generations/epochs, no lazy writes/locks. Capture inside the archive's consistent snapshot and compare from fresh transactions during generation/before delivery. This helper alone does not cover legacy-only/no-store Shopify facts or other providers. Root approved a separate global `Workspace.export_privacy_revision` in0053, owned by the export package, and `app.lifecycle.privacy.bump_workspace_export_revision(db, workspace_id)`. The shared0053 dependency is now included and every commerce invalidation calls its counter helper atomically. Other source hooks and archive capture/fresh checks remain in the export owner’s package; this branch alone does not claim the whole export privacy boundary complete. No already-delivered bytes can be recalled.

Lifecycle registry explicitly classifies both new tables as workspace-owned, scalar metadata only. Publication JSON is deliberately excluded from generic export; the export owner adds its reviewed domain serializer separately. No unrestricted JSON export is authorized.
